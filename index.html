Letterstorm: Cyberpunk Typing Simulator & WPM Test

Boost your typing speed and WPM with Letterstorm, a high-octane cyberpunk arcade typing game. Test your reflexes and survive the neon grid. Play free in your browser.

Master Your Typing Speed

Improve your touch typing practice and test your keyboard reaction times against dynamic incoming words. Earn combo multipliers, unleash devastating power-ups, and reach your highest words-per-minute (WPM) record in our retro-grid arcade simulator.
